# EXP-029: decision brief (2026-11-01)

The original specification (`2026-11-01-exp029-weighting-spec.md`) is **unchanged and unregistered**. This brief
checks it and proposes a separate alternative design, EXP-029b. Nothing here registers or activates anything.

**How the numbers were produced:**
- Sample sizes: `research/power.py`.
- Discordance and composite correlations: `research/exp029_feasibility.py`, on the 206 post-seal V1 readings. It uses
  source scores only and no returns, so no outcome was looked at.
- Tests: `research/test_exp029_feasibility.py`.

## 1. What exactly is being compared?

The direction call of the V1 composite (above 50 means UP) under four weightings of the same recorded source scores:

| Id | Weighting |
|---|---|
| W0 | Production: base weight × confidence × boost |
| W1 | Equal base weights, confidence kept |
| W2 | Base weights with confidence fixed at 1 and no boost |
| W3 | The frozen adaptive shadow proposal |

The outcome is BTC's 24 h direction.

## 2. What decision would the result enable?

Whether to change the production weights. The two layers in question are the hand-set base weights (W1) and the
confidence/boost layer (W2). The third question is whether the adaptive proposal (W3) beats both.

## 3. How many informative observations are needed?

**122 days on which the two calls differ, per comparison.**
- **Settings:** exact sign test, P = 0.65 under the alternative, power 0.80, two-sided, Holm with m = 3.
- **Verified:**
  - power is 0.819 at n = 122 and 0.787 at n = 121;
  - the test's actual size under the null is 0.0142, below α/3 = 0.0167.
- **The number is consistent with the documented assumptions.**

## 4. How long would collection take?

| | W1 vs W0 | W2 vs W0 |
|---|---|---|
| Share of readings whose call differs from W0 | 12.6% (26 / 206) | 3.4% (7 / 206) |
| Spearman correlation with W0 | 0.955 | 0.992 |
| Days needed at 1 eligible unit per day (requires server collection) | 122 / 0.126 ≈ **970 days (2.7 years)** | 122 / 0.034 ≈ **3,600 days (~10 years)** |
| Days needed with browser-only collection (2 eligible units in 21 days) | effectively never | effectively never |

**Caveat:** the archive stores no confidence or boost per reading, so W0 here uses the default weights. W3 cannot be
estimated offline.

**Conclusion: EXP-029 as specified is impractical.** The weightings rarely disagree, so the discordant-days design
collects information extremely slowly.

## 5. Minimum collection quality required (any design)

- **Server-side collection active:** quiquandon-oss/PulseWorker#3. Browser-only collection produced 2 eligible days in
  21.
- **Daily-unit eligibility of at least 90%:** a reading within 60 min after 00:00 UTC.
- **Completeness:** at least 50% (no `below_floor`).
- **Reproducible provenance:** server recomputation equals the stored value (PulseWorker#2 / #3).
- **Monthly report of these rates.** If eligibility stays below 70% for two consecutive months, the experiment is
  paused and reported; nothing is changed after the fact.

## 6. What result would justify changing the weights?

- **Under EXP-029b (below):** a Holm-significant difference in the 24 h rank correlation with BTC's return, of at least
  0.10 in favour of Wk.
- **Before any production change:** a separate controlled-release plan, because EXP-025 and EXP-027 use the
  composite as a covariate.

## 7. What result would justify abandoning?

- **Collection quality:** the floor in §5 is missed for two consecutive months.
- **Futility:** at the checkpoint, the 95% CI of the difference excludes 0.10 in favour of Wk, so any gain is smaller
  than decision-relevant.
- **Already answerable from structure, with no outcome data:** W2 versus W0. The composites correlate at 0.992, so
  their correlations with any outcome can differ by at most √(2(1−0.992)) ≈ 0.13.
  - **Implication:** the confidence/boost layer barely changes the signal. Whether to keep it is a simplicity decision,
    not a predictive one.
  - **Recommendation:** drop W2 from the confirmatory family. This raises the per-comparison α for W1 and W3.

## Recommended alternative: EXP-029b (PROPOSED, separate, not registered)

**Same population, unit and outcome as EXP-029, with a different statistic.**
- **Statistic:** Steiger's test for two dependent correlations, comparing Spearman ρ(Wk composite, 24 h log return)
  with ρ(W0 composite, return) on **all** eligible days, not only the days where calls differ.
- **Sample size** (`n_dependent_correlation_difference`, Monte Carlo-checked):
  - **Settings:** minimum effect Δρ = 0.10 (0.20 vs 0.10), power 0.80, Holm with m = 2 (W1, W3) or m = 3.
  - **Required days at r12 = 0.955 (W1):**
    - Holm m = 3: **95 days**;
    - Holm m = 2: computed at registration from the composites' observed correlation.
  - **Simulation:** 4,000 runs at n = 95 gave power 0.828, and the size under the null was 0.015 (α/3 = 0.0167).
- **Duration:** about 3–4 months after server collection starts, against 2.7–10 years for the discordant-days design.
- **Checkpoint:** one confirmatory analysis at the computed n, with no interim look, and r12 fixed from the first 30
  days' composites (scores only) before any outcome is joined.

Choose one:

2. **Recommended:** register EXP-029b, with W2 dropped as structurally answered, once PulseWorker#3 is collecting.
